Transparent background. Style: thick dark green outline (#1E3A24, about 6 px), flat fills with one soft lighter shade, no gradients, no textures, no shadows on the ground. One object per cell, nothing touching the cell edges.

What I do when a sheet arrives

  1. The October pipeline: recolour to the exact hexes, cut by cell, measure each object, upscale.
  2. Give each object its base point, so it stands on its slot and grows from there.
  3. Check scale against Doum in a line-up, and every object on the dark theme (rule 6).
  4. Send you a contact sheet before anything goes in the app; redo cells you don’t like.

Why split parts

A palm sways from its crown and a flower opens from its head. If the trunk and crown come as separate stickers, they animate naturally; one flat picture can only bob as a whole. Sheet X is those parts, plus the small effects (drops, splash, puff, sparkle) so every moment on the Motion board has real art.
The lesson from drawing this canvas: a round canopy with fruit dots and a curved nest under it reads as a smiling face. The style block forbids faces, and the contact sheet catches the rest.
