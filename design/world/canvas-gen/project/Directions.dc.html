Mixes worth a thought

Tree, with a small world at its feet. B as drawn already does this: the tree is the hero, and stones, flowers, a pond, a bench and a hive gather round it. Most of the planet’s variety at the tree’s clarity.
Choose the tree. B and C are the same code with a different trunk. People could pick a tree or a palm on day one, like they pick a character.
The planet, then the tree. A already ends in a great tree at level 35. The tree could be the first year and the planet what it stands on later, but that means building both.

The same in every direction

Nothing earned ever goes. Quiet days come back with one habit. Rest, pause and freezes never count. Doum is never sad. No new notification, nothing sold, private, can be hidden. It lives one tap from Profile and only shows up on the Grid as a moment.
So the phone boards and the build plan on this canvas hold for whichever you pick: they show the planet only as the example.
