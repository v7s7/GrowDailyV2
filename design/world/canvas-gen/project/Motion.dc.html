Motion

Every animation, timed

The world is calm. It moves when you did something, says so once, and goes still. These are the moments, in the order people meet them, with timings in milliseconds, the haptic that goes with each, and what Reduce Motion shows instead.

Only recovery is animated
The world never wilts in front of anyone. A thirsty world is simply already thirsty when you arrive; only watering, growing and blooming move.
React, then rest
Rule 1 for Doum holds for the world: one moment at a time, never stacked. Ambient motion runs only on the world’s own page and stops after 20 s without a touch.
Three speeds
Touch feedback 150 to 250 ms. A moment 600 to 1,800 ms. Colour and light 1,200 ms for thirst, 2,000 ms for day to night. Pops overshoot (ease out back), colour eases in and out.
Reduce Motion
No movement at all: every moment becomes a 200 ms crossfade to its end state, ambient motion is off, the spin keeps working under the finger but without inertia.

How it moves in Flutter

Flutter’s own tools first: the world as layers (sky, ground, items, Doum, glow), each item a widget at its slot, a single AnimationController per moment, colours through Color.lerp, pops with Curves.easeOutBack. Doum keeps his existing Sprout widget, hop and celebrate. Static layers sit in a RepaintBoundary so only what moves repaints.
Rive is the upgrade path for Doum alone: its meshes can bend the existing ChatGPT poses (a blink, the can tilting, a breath) and its state machines are why Duolingo can animate characters at scale. The world stays Flutter-native; Lottie, already in the app, stays for rare celebrations. iOS Reduce Motion must be read through the app’s own prefersReducedMotion, not MediaQuery.disableAnimations alone.

The widget, and the Profile card

The Home Screen widget cannot run free animation: the app renders a picture per state, and since iOS 17 a change between timeline entries can transition for up to 2 seconds. The Profile card stays still too: it shows the world as it is, and only the world’s page moves.
Sounds: none. The app has none today, and the moments are built to work silent.
