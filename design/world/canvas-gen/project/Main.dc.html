Doum’s planet

Doum stays exactly as he is. He gets a small planet of his own that fills up with what your days have grown: one new thing every level, a flower for every medal, a pearl for every week in a row. Nothing earned ever leaves it. When days go quiet it gets a little thirsty, and the first habit of the day waters it.

MY PICK: THE ONE THAT SCORES 5 OF 5

G. Doum’s oasis «واحة دوم»

Doum farms a small round world, and at its crown stands one date palm that grows with you from the first day. Your habits are his water and your levels are his seeds; he plants and waters while you watch. Medals ripen the palm’s dates (hababou, khalal, rutab, tamr), weeks in a row drop pearls in the sea, quiet days rest it without drying it.
It takes the best row from each direction: Doum working it (F), one palm you can read at a glance (B and C), a year of new things around it (A), the Gulf in every part. And because nothing on it can be bought, it is something people can see: the app icon grows into the palm, and a small oasis sits beside every name in a room (the Seen by others board).
Wave 1 is the palm, the world and the Profile card, drawn in code like this canvas: about the size of building the tree alone. The rest comes in small waves.

Three layers, and only one of them ever goes back

1. Grows: your level
Permanent
Each level from 1 to 35 adds one thing: sprouts, a well, a lemon tree, Doum’s house, the sea, a boat, a palm grove, the great tree. After 35 the great tree grows every 5 levels, to 100. It is built from the highest level ever reached, so an undo, which can lower a level today, never takes anything back.
2. Blooms: medals and weeks
Permanent
Every medal plants a flower, and its family picks which one: five kinds, twenty in all. Every 7 days in a row drops a pearl into the sea. Today the app logs that week and only shows it in the monthly story; here it becomes something you keep.
3. Water: your last few days
Comes back
The only part that changes back. A day with habits owed and nothing done makes the planet a little thirsty, three steps at most. The first habit done today waters it back in full. Rest days, paused habits, days a freeze covered and days with nothing owed never count.

You asked: should it die a little when things go badly?

Three ways to answer. The research is one-sided: every app that started with death moved away from it, and the best-loved ones never had it. Gentle thirst keeps the pull you want without the guilt.

OPTION A
Nothing changes
Like Finch and Neko Atsume. The safest choice, and it loses the pull you asked for: the planet looks the same whether you came or not.
Finch: the bird stays home instead of going out. Neko Atsume: no penalties at all.
OPTION B · RECOMMENDED
A little thirsty, never dead
Paler soil, flowers closed, the spring low, the kite down. Nothing is removed. One habit brings it all back, with Doum watering. Copy names the planet’s state, never the person.
Plant Nanny 2 wilts and perks up after one glass. Duolingo’s one-lesson rule: +3.3% day-14 retention. Slack days: goals met up to 40% more often.
OPTION C · NOT RECOMMENDED
Things die or are lost
Forest’s stumps, Habitica’s lost levels, the first Plant Nanny and Tamagotchi. Plant Nanny and Tamagotchi both moved away from death. A broken record makes people quit rather than restart. For a prayer app it reads as a verdict on worship.
CU Boulder, seven studies on broken streaks. Tamagotchi pets run away instead of dying since 2009.
Two ways to draw B, and the admin page picks
Thirsty (left): paler soil and leaves, flowers closed, the spring low. Closest to what you asked for. Quiet, not dry (right): colours stay healthy; flowers close, the kite comes down, Doum waits with his can. One habit opens everything in both.
The second round of research leans to quiet. In the UbiFit Garden study (CHI 2008) a garden left sparse but healthy, never wilted, kept people active over the holidays; in Fish’n’Steps a sad fish made some people stop opening the app. And where springs have really dried up, a drying spring is not a neutral picture.
Build both and put the switch on the «دوم» admin page. Start with quiet; if it feels too soft, flip it to thirsty without a release.

Option B, exactly
  1. Look back only over days that had habits owed (the owed-day rule). Skip rest days «راحة», paused habits, days a streak freeze covered and days with nothing owed.
  2. Count the most recent of those days, in a row, where nothing at all was done. That count is the thirst: 0 to 3, never more.
  3. Yesterday is not counted until 10:00, the same cutoff that still lets it be filled in.
  4. One square done today, any habit, any kind, sets today to 0 at once. A day that earns its streak point adds sparkles on top.
  5. It never singles out a habit. Only a day with nothing done counts, so a missed prayer on a day with other habits done changes nothing on the planet.

Rules it keeps

Doum is never sad. On a thirsty day he holds the can and smiles; the planet shows the quiet days, he never does.
No notifications from the oasis, ever. It speaks only inside the app.
Growth is never sold. No gold and no money waters it, grows it or skips a quiet day. Gold, which is only earned, builds the made things (a bench, lanterns, Doum’s house); money never touches the oasis.
No reward claims for worship. It is Doum’s garden, not a picture of the hereafter: no «a palm in Jannah» lines, no verses.
Seen only where the person chooses: beside their name in rooms (one switch), a card they share. Never ranked, never about worship, and quiet days are never shown to anyone else.
Can be hidden from Settings, the same way ledge Doum can.
No animals. The world is trees, plants, water, buildings and objects, things without a soul, which every view of drawing in Islam allows. Butterflies, birds and bees became a kite, lanterns, a basket of lemons and a telescope.

One rule bends, and it needs your yes

Rule 2 in POSES.md says nothing shows a missed day, and the sprout’s mood code says the same in its comments. Option B lets the planet show quiet days. Doum himself still never does: the most he does is hold a watering can.
The app already has a plant that grows: the app icon goes seedling, sprout, grown, bloom over full days. It stays as it is. The planet does not replace it, and the two never contradict each other because neither ever shrinks.
The pose he uses on a thirsty day is the one drawn for “Grow Daily itself”: habits_watering_plant, smiling, never worried.

Six decisions for you

1. Which direction?
Recommended: G, Doum’s oasis. Or A to F (the Seven directions board). Whichever it is, Doum stays as he is and lives beside it: he waters it and plants each new thing.
2. Quiet days: A, B or C?
Recommended: B, never dead, one habit brings it back, drawn quiet or thirsty by a switch on the admin page. Bends rule 2 for the world only.
3. Its name
If it is the planet: «كوكب دوم» (your word), «جزيرة دوم» (an island: sea, pearls and palms fit it) or «حديقة دوم». Otherwise «شجرة دوم», «نخلة دوم» or «مزرعة دوم».
4. Reminders
Decided 3 October: no notifications from the oasis at all. It speaks only inside the app.
5. The art
Recommended: drawn in code like this canvas (crisp at any size, thirst fades smoothly, tiny download). ChatGPT sticker sheets in Doum’s style for a later polish; prompts are on the Build board.
6. Seen by others, and the widget
The icon growing into the palm, the oasis beside names in rooms (one switch to hide it), a card to share; and a widget of the world without Doum (rule 7). Recommended: icon and rooms in wave 2, the rest in wave 3.
