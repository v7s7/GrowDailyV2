Should a neglected world visibly die? No.

Apps that started with death moved away from it: Plant Nanny 2, and Tamagotchi since 2009. The best-rated companions never had it. The research says a broken record makes people quit, not try again. The only apps where loss works, Forest and SleepTown, take one session the person chose to start and never touch the rest of the world.

What the best do instead: earned things are permanent, a reversible mood shows recent days, one small action restores it, rest leaves the world alone, and reminders invite rather than warn.

Gaps: no published A/B test compares decay with no decay for a companion world. Finch’s retention figures are third-party estimates. There is no peer-reviewed data on streaks in Muslim apps. Habitica’s punishment complaints are reviews, not measurements.
