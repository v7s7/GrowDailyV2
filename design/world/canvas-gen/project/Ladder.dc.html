The growth ladder

One new thing every level

Levels 1 to 35 each add one thing. After that a level takes weeks, so the great tree takes over and grows every 5 levels, to 100. The chapters follow the ranks the app already has, so a rank-up and a new chapter land on the same day. Everything is built from the highest level ever reached.

1 week
to level 5: a well, then a palm shoot
1 month
to level 10: the first fruit tree
4 months
to level 20: the sea arrives
1 year
to level 35: the great tree

Paced for about 6 habits a day, roughly 150 XP. The curve is the app’s own: level n needs n × 100 XP, so level 35 is 59,500 XP in all and level 100 is 495,000.

Medal flowers: the family picks the flower

Every one of the 20 medals plants one flower in the grass, the day it is earned. Five families, five kinds, four of each: a set people like to finish. Tap a flower on the planet to see its medal and the date.

Lavender خزامى
Grid medals «الشبكة»: 1, 100, 500, 2,000 done squares
Sunflower دوار الشمس
Streak medals «بدون انقطاع»: 7, 30, 100, 365 days
Daisy أقحوان
Completion medals «الثبات»: 50, 500, 2,000, 5,000
Tulip توليب
Level medals «الصعود»: levels 10, 25, 50, 100
Wild rose ورد بري
Long-breath medals «النَفَس الطويل»: 150, 200, 250, 300 days

Pearls: a week in a row

Each time the streak reaches 7, 14, 21 and on, a pearl lands in the sea: a nod to the old pearling days. Today the app logs this week as a milestone and shows it only in the monthly story. Before level 20 there is no sea yet, so pearls wait in a shell by the well and pour in when it comes.

It changes even when nothing grows

Night
Doum’s own hours from the admin page: asleep after 21:00, awake at 4:00. Stars, the moon, lanterns glowing.
Ramadan
A crescent every night and Doum with his fanous. No food poses by day (rule 4). Ramadan 1448 starts about 8 February 2027.
Eid
Lights strung between the palm and the house, his Eid thobe.
Winter
December to February: a cooler sky and his fireside looks, like the splash.
Summer
June to August: a bigger sun, and the dates ripen.
