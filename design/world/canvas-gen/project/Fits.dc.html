How it all fits: XP, gold and the oasis

You already earn three things for every habit. With the oasis each gets one clear job. Nothing new to earn, nothing changes in how XP or gold are paid, and nothing can be bought with money.

XP grows it

الـXP يكبّرها
Everything that grows comes with your level: the palm at the crown, trees, flowers, grass, the spring, the sea. Doum plants each one himself. Growth can’t be bought, not with gold and not with money, so a big oasis always means someone kept going.
XP is paid exactly as today: about 20 per habit, level n needs n × 100. It grows from the highest level ever reached.

Gold builds in it

الذهب يبني فيها
Everything that is made you build with gold: Doum’s house, the well, a bench, lanterns, a kite, a boat. Each opens at a level, then costs gold, and you choose where it goes. The oasis looks complete without any of them; they are how you make it yours.
Gold is paid exactly as today: about 8 per habit and 4 per task. It is only ever earned, never sold.
Medals ripen the dates
Each medal family is a bunch on the palm; each tier ripens it: hababou, khalal, rutab, tamr.
7 days in a row: a pearl
Every 7, 14, 21 days of streak, a pearl lands in the sea.
Any habit today: watered
The first square of the day and Doum waters it. Quiet days rest it, never ruin it.
