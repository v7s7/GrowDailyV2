What iOS allows
A widget cannot run free animation. The app renders the world to a picture whenever its state changes (a habit, a level, night falls) and hands WidgetKit a timeline: morning, noon, evening, night. iOS 17+ crossfades between pictures, so thirsty to fresh still looks like a change, not a jump.
Simple shapes read best at this size: the tree and the palm hold up better than the busy planet. That is one more reason to weigh direction against the widget.
