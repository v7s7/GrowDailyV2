The deliberate "partly done" mark
Drawn at the real floor size, 30pt, with the app's own fills. The glyph inside renders at size x 0.5, so 15pt. Each option is shown beside a finished square, because telling those two apart at a glance is the whole job.
Option A
Half fill, no glyph
The fill is the mark. Nothing to learn, nothing to squint at.

Cost: it is the same mechanism a counted habit uses, so a bare half fill and a "2 of 4" differ only by the number.
Option B
Diagonal half
Still "half", but on a diagonal, which a counted fill never is. The two part-done meanings stay apart without a legend.

Cost: one new shape to meet.
Option C
Hollow tick
Same tick, drawn thin: "done, with a caveat".

Cost: at 15pt a thin tick and a thick tick are one squint apart, and the colour is already carrying that difference.
Option D
Half tick
The tick's first stroke solid, its long stroke left unfinished. Literally a half-finished done mark, at the same weight as the real one.

Cost: the dashed tail is 3pt of detail; it survives 30pt but it is the finest thing on the board.
Option E
Wavy half
Your suggestion, rendered honestly at 30pt: the wave has about 3pt of amplitude.

Cost: at this size it reads as a soft or mis-rendered edge, not a wave, and it still collides with the counted fill.
Today
What it is now
A timelapse dial.

Cost: it says "in progress", which is a different claim from "partly done", and it now collides with timer habits, which draw a real timer glyph.
